Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

01. How does 'Made To Order' work?

Unlike fast food buffets, Master Chef Kartheek slow-cooks every single Dum Biryani handi tray specifically for your booking. Orders must be placed by 2:00 PM the prior day so that fresh marinades can rest overnight.

02. Why is daily capacity limited to 25 Handi Trays?

Authentic Dum Pukht requires 4 hours of gentle slow cooking over low flame with dough-sealed vessels. Limiting production to 25 trays guarantees uncompromising royal flavor, portion generousness, and culinary excellence in every batch.

03. Is the poultry and meat 100% Halal?

Yes! All chicken, mutton, and beef are 100% Zabiha Halal certified. We maintain strictly separated, dedicated cooking vessels and preparation zones for all distinct meat cuts.

04. How many people does one Handi Tray serve?

Each generous Handi Tray serves 4 to 5 adults comfortably. It contains 1.6 to 1.8 kg of marinated protein, 1 kg of raw aged basmati rice, boiled eggs, roasted cashews, fried onions, raita, and complimentary dessert.
